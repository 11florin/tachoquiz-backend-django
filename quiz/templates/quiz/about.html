{% translate "About this app" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Tacho Quiz was created by a professional driver who understands first-hand how challenging the early stages of a professional driving career can be. When I first entered the industry, I found the learning process overwhelming - especially when it came to understanding driving times, rest periods, tachograph rules, and preparing for the CPC qualification. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} This application was built with a simple purpose: to make learning easier, clearer, and more accessible for drivers at any stage of their journey. Tacho Quiz helps drivers revise essential knowledge through short, focused questions that can be completed anywhere - during breaks, at home, or while preparing for CPC assessments. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} The quiz format encourages repetition and confidence-building, allowing users to strengthen their understanding of key regulations without pressure. Whether you are a new driver navigating tachograph rules or an experienced professional refreshing your knowledge, this app supports you in becoming safer, more informed, and better prepared. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Tacho Quiz was created by a driver, for drivers - with the goal of making the path toward CPC certification and professional development smoother and more approachable. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Disclaimer: Tacho Quiz is an independent educational application intended to support professional drivers in the United Kingdom. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the DVSA or any other UK government authority. The questions and explanations are independently created for learning and revision purposes and are not official DVSA or CPC examination questions. While reasonable efforts are made to keep the information accurate and up to date, regulations and official guidance may change. Users should always check the latest official UK legislation and guidance before relying on the information for professional or legal compliance. {% endblocktranslate %}
